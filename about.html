About us

A family business, built one event at a time.

Romero's Party Needs started the way a lot of family businesses do: with a set of folding tables, a borrowed truck, and a favor for a relative's birthday. Word got around the neighborhood faster than the tables could be cleaned and stacked, and what began as helping out family turned into helping out the whole community.

Today the business still runs the way it started — hands-on, and involved in every event from setup to the last chair packed away. Every tent, table, and signage order still gets checked by someone in the family before it goes out the door.

What hasn't changed is the reason for doing it: making someone else's celebration easier, so they get to actually be at their own party instead of running it.

Our partner in hosting

For hosting, wedding coordination, and full event organizing, we work hand-in-hand with South Flaneur Events — a company run by family and close friends. They're not part of Romero's Party Needs itself, but the partnership means you can book rentals and a full hosting team through one call.
